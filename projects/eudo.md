Eudo runs experiments inside organizations and aggregates the learnings. This should help organizations learn more quickly, both from their own work and the work of other organizations. Hopefully this will make them more effectively accomplish their goals. You'd think experiments would be widely practiced inside large organizations, but for the most part they aren't. We'd like to change that.
