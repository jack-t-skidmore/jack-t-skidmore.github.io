Eudo runs experiments inside organizations and aggregates the learnings. We think this will help organizations learn more quickly from their own work, and the work of other organizations. This should result in organizations being more effective at accomplishing their goals. You'd think experiments would be widely practiced inside large organizations, but for the most part they aren't. We'd like to change that.
